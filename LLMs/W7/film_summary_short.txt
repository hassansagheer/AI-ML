Wealthy businessman Yash disowns his adopted son Rahul for marrying Anjali, a woman from a lower class, leading Rahul to move to London with his new family. Ten years later, Rahul's younger brother Rohan learns of the estrangement and travels to London under a false pretext to reunite them. Rohan infiltrates Rahul's life using an alias, bonding with his family and developing a relationship with Anjali's sister, Pooja, while concealing his true identity. After Rohan reveals himself, his parents arrive in London, leading to an emotional reunion between Rahul and his mother, Nandini. However, Yash's anger over the past and Rohan's deception remains unresolved as the family returns to India following a death.
